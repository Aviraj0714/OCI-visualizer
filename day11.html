05Skill Building

Exam Question Pattern Explorer

Oracle exam questions follow recognizable patterns. Learning to spot them saves real time.

🔑 Keyword Recognition

Words like "MUST", "ALWAYS", "MINIMUM cost", or "MOST secure" signal exactly which dimension the question is testing — read them twice.

✂️ Elimination Technique

Eliminate options that are factually wrong first, then choose between the remaining two based on the specific constraint given.

📖 Scenario Analysis

Underline the actual constraint (compliance, cost, latency) — many wrong answers are technically valid but don't fit the stated constraint.

⏱ Time Management

Budget roughly 90 seconds/question; flag and move on if stuck past that — don't let one question consume your buffer.

Try it: "A company needs the MOST cost-effective way to retain compliance logs for 7 years, rarely accessed."
